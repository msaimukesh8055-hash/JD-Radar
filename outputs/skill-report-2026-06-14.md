## Key Takeaways

1. **Roadmapping and strategy are the connective tissue.** 11/27 JDs explicitly call for roadmap ownership and 9/27 for product strategy/vision — these two, plus stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration (7 each), form the "default PM skillset" at every MNC regardless of AI focus. If you're prepping for these roles, this is the baseline to be fluent in.

2. **AI is now a parallel track, not a bolt-on.** Generative AI and Agentic AI each appear in 5/27 JDs — concentrated heavily in Google, Salesforce, and ServiceNow postings. Notably, **Salesforce's entire India PM slate we found is AI-first** (all three postings mention agentic/generative AI), suggesting Salesforce is hiring a distinct cohort of "AI-native" PMs rather than retrofitting existing roles.

3. **"Technical PM" is its own lane, led by consulting/services firms.** HashedIn by Deloitte's two postings and Microsoft's Principal PM role explicitly want cloud platforms (GCP/AWS/Azure), APIs, and Agile tooling — this looks like a distinct technical-PM track aimed at engineering-adjacent candidates, separate from the strategy-heavy Amazon/Walmart roles.

4. **Process-heavy roles cluster at consulting firms.** PwC and the HashedIn/Deloitte postings emphasize Agile ceremonies, backlog grooming, and user-story writing more than any other companies — if you're coming from a Scrum/Agile background, these are likely your best fits.

5. **Smaller/scaleup companies (Karat, Toast, KRAFTON, BitGo) ask for more hands-on, "do it yourself" skills** — AI tool fluency, data analysis, experimentation, and lifecycle/process ownership — versus the large-org "manage through others" skills at Amazon/Walmart.

6. **My read:** if a candidate had to pick 3 things to be sharp on for *this specific batch* of MNC roles, I'd say: (1) roadmap ownership + stakeholder communication (universal), (2) a working vocabulary in Generative AI / Agentic AI / LLMs even if not hands-on technical (clearly trending at Google/Salesforce/ServiceNow), and (3) comfort with data analysis tools (Tableau/SQL-adjacent) since several postings call this out as differentiating.

## Methodology & Limitations

- **Search queries** combined company + role + location + site, e.g. `Product Manager AI Google India jobs`, `Amazon Senior Product Manager India site:amazon.jobs`, `ServiceNow Product Manager India site:boards.greenhouse.io`, `Deloitte Product Manager India site:linkedin.com/jobs`, varied across cities and seniority.
- **Most productive sources:** company career pages (amazon.jobs, careers.cognizant.com) and Greenhouse boards (Karat, Toast, KRAFTON) returned full JD text; WebSearch snippets reliably surfaced responsibilities + qualifications for the rest.
- **Blocked / expired for full fetch:** LinkedIn and most individual Microsoft/Salesforce/Mastercard job pages 301-redirect or return 410 (expired); Google careers pages are JS-rendered/truncated. For these, analysis relied on WebSearch snippet text.
- **Dedup & exclusions:** 35 postings collected, all URLs unique. **8 excluded** for having no usable description (only title/company/location, or "listed alongside other roles" with no skill signal): Deloitte Manager–Product Architect CL5, Deloitte Senior Product Manager, PwC ACI AI Product Manager, PwC Integration Product Manager – Manager, Mastercard Senior Product Manager, Mastercard Manager–Product Management, Microsoft Product Manager II, PayPal Product Manager. **27 valid JDs analyzed.**
- **Counting:** each canonical skill counted once per distinct JD (by URL), never per mention. Synonyms merged to canonical forms (e.g. Gen AI/GenAI → "Generative AI"; LLM/LLMs → "LLMs"). Skills were *inferred* from sentence-level context, not just explicit "skills" lists.
- **Recency:** most source pages do not expose a posting date; one Deloitte listing (excluded for thin content) was dated April 2026. Postings were retrieved live during this run (2026-06-14), so all included JDs were active/visible at run time, but exact post dates are largely unavailable from these sources — a known limitation.
- **Caveat:** only ~7-8 of 27 are full-text JDs; the rest are rich multi-sentence snippets. Inferred per-company skill lists and the aggregate counts are therefore **conservative** — true skill demand (especially deeper technical skills) is likely higher. No postings were fabricated; every entry maps to a real retrieved URL.
